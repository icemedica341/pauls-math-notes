# Paul's Math Notes

**Live site:** https://icemedica341.github.io/pauls-math-notes/

A personal study record covering algebra through trigonometry — definitions
clarified, procedures summarised, and hard-won gotchas written down so they
stick.

## Origin

These notes were written as a Google Doc over February–March 2025 while
working through Paul's Online Math Notes (and Sullivan's *Algebra &
Trigonometry* for the trig sections). In October 2025 the document was
converted to Markdown to future-proof it out of a proprietary format, and
rendered as a static site with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/).

## What it covers

- **General notes** — thoughts on learning maths
- **List of errors** — recurring mistakes, recorded to break the habit
- **Technical notes** — the "why can/can't we do this" gotchas school glosses over
- **Algebra** — clarifications and summaries per section of Paul's Online Math Notes
- **Trigonometry** — essentials gathered from Sullivan's *Algebra and Trigonometry*

Calculus I is intentionally absent: proving the rules pulled that work into
Jupyter notebooks instead.
